AnimationPointerUVs/glTF/AnimationPointerUVs.gltf
    Its animation has two channels with the pointer
    /materials/18/pbrMetallicRoughness/metallicRoughnessTexture/extensions/KHR_texture_transform/rotation, among
    others, which KHR_animation_pointer forbids: different channels of one animation must not have identical pointers.
    lib/gltf refuses the asset as InvalidData.
