Release automation

Proposal · Platform team · Last edited by Dana Whitfield

Pipeline

Every push builds and runs the tests. A green build deploys to staging and then releases. A red build notifies its author, and nothing deploys.

Reviews

A change starts as a draft. Once its author asks for review, it needs one approval from the owning team before it merges. Reviewers are assigned by the paths that the change touches, and a change that touches several teams' paths waits for each of them.

Drafts are not built, so that early pushes do not queue behind finished work.
