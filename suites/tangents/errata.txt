USDShaderBallForGltf/glTF/USDShaderBallForGltf.gltf
USDShaderBallForGltf/glTF-Binary/USDShaderBallForGltf.glb
    The reference's BuildNeighborsFast sorts its edges by their lower vertex, then sorts each run of one lower vertex
    by the upper vertex and each run of one edge by triangle, closing a run when the next one starts. The last run of
    each pass is never closed, so the edges of the vertex that is the lower end of the edges last in the first sort
    stay unsorted, and pairing, which looks only at the run of equal edges that follows an edge, misses some of their
    neighbors. Two triangles that share such an edge then fall into two groups, at a few corners near the end of a
    primitive: 2 to 12 of each of these assets'. With those two runs sorted, the reference's tangents equal lib/mesh's
    at every corner of the corpus.
