# Layers that lib/usd reads as the USD Core Specification has them and the reference, Pixar's OpenUSD, does not. An
# entry lists its layers, a key per line, followed by the indented reason: what the reference does and why it loses.

usd-assets/test_assets/foundation/stage_configuration/timeCodesPerSecond/timeCodesPerSecond_-1.usda#stage
    The root layer's `timeCodesPerSecond` is -1. The reference scales its sublayers' times by -1/24, which reverses
    time. The specification has a scale that is not positive be a composition error that leaves the retiming as it
    is (usd-core/#sublayer-offset-adjustment), which is what lib/usd does.

usd-spec-supplemental/releases/1.0.1/composition/tests/assets/ErrorArcCycle_root/usda/root.usd#stage
    These layers hold arcs that are cycles, as a prim that references its parent. The specification has such an arc
    ignored (usd-core/#composition-errors), the results that the specification's own composition test expects of
    these layers have no opinion from one, and the reference's composition agrees when it is asked for one prim
    (Pcp.Cache.ComputePrimIndex): for </AnotherParent/AnotherChild> of root.usd it gives the prim's own spec and the
    spec of the prim that it references in model.usd, and reports the cycle. The reference's stage (Usd.Stage.Open)
    of the same layer gives that prim a third opinion, the spec of its parent, which the arc that it reported as a
    cycle brings, and a child composed from it. lib/usd composes what the specification, its test and the reference's
    own composition give.

usd-spec-supplemental/releases/1.0.1/composition/tests/assets/ErrorInvalidInstanceTargetPath_root/pcp.txt
    The test's classes connect an attribute to a prim of each of their instances, and the expected results differ
    from lib/usd's in the connections of four attributes. They are the results of the validation that OpenUSD's
    composition applies when it is asked to (Pcp.Cache.ComputeAttributeConnectionPaths), which rejects a connection
    that a class makes to an instance of the class. The specification states no such rule, and OpenUSD's stage does
    not apply it: Usd.Attribute.GetConnections maps each path through its arcs and keeps those that map, which is
    what lib/usd does, and the two agree on every one of these attributes (the stages of this test's layers pass).
    For </ConnectionToLocalClass/Instance_1.x> the expected results and OpenUSD 26.11's composition give no
    connection, and its stage and lib/usd give </ConnectionToLocalClass/Instance_2.y>. For
    </FaceRig/BrowRig/LBrow/BrowInnUDPosBrowInnUDNeg_sculpt.amount> the three differ: the expected results give
    </FaceRig/BrowRig/Anim/LBrow.InnUD>, a path that a relocate of the root layer has moved the prim from, OpenUSD
    26.11's composition gives none and reports both authored paths, and its stage and lib/usd give
    </FaceRig/Anim/Face/Eyes/RBrow.InnUD>. So the expected results are not those of the reference that the suite
    runs either. lib/usd reports the paths that do not map, on each of the prims that the expected results report.

usd-spec-supplemental/releases/1.0.1/composition/tests/assets/ErrorOpinionAtRelocationSource_root/root.usd#stage
usd-spec-supplemental/releases/1.0.1/composition/tests/assets/ErrorOpinionAtRelocationSource_root/usda/root.usd#stage
    The root layer has a spec at </VariantAtRelocateSource/Child/Child2>, the source of a relocate to
    </VariantAtRelocateSource/Sibling>, and the spec lists a variant set, `child2`. The specification has a spec at
    a relocate's source ignored (usd-core/#relocates), the results that the specification's own composition test
    expects give </VariantAtRelocateSource/Sibling> no variant selection, and the reference agrees everywhere but in
    one call: it reports the spec as "an invalid opinion at the relocation source path ... which will be ignored",
    its prim stack for the prim leaves the spec out, and its resolved `variantSetNames` of the prim
    (Usd.Prim.GetMetadata) is empty. Its list of the prim's variant sets (Usd.Prim.GetVariantSets().GetNames())
    still names `child2`, with no selection, because that call reads the field at every node of the prim's index,
    the placeholder at the relocate's source too. lib/usd lists the variant sets that the prim's opinions give.

usd-spec-supplemental/releases/1.0.1/composition/tests/assets/ExpressionsInReferences_root/usda/root.usd#stage
    The asset paths of these layers' references and payloads are variable expressions, as @`"${REF}.usd"`@, which the
    reference evaluates against the layers' `expressionVariables`. The specification leaves variable expressions out:
    its list of known issues says that they are "not yet covered" (usd-core/#correlation-with-openusd-version-and-
    known-issues). lib/usd takes an asset path as it is written, finds no layer at the expression's text, and reports
    the arc as an error, which is what the specification has an arc to a layer that cannot be read do.

usd-spec-supplemental/releases/1.0.1/file_formats/tests/assets/text/usda/geometryattributes.usda
    The layer's header is `#usda 1.0.123123123`. The specification's grammar gives a header's patch version any
    number of digits (usd-core/#layer, LayerHeader), and the layer is one of the specification's own tests of the
    text format. The reference refuses it: "Unable to parse layer version from '1.0.123123123'". It reads a patch
    version of three digits, as `1.0.234`, so the bound is its own. lib/usd reads the layer, whose content is of
    version 1.0.
